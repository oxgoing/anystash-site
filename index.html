AnyStash

AnyStash means what it sounds like: a place to stash what your Shortcuts need to keep—typed values and files, locations, event sessions, and Live Activities—so what you run stays visible, and what you save stays reusable.

Not a calendar app: Event Sessions are start/stop activities with history, not appointments. Not only a parked-car pin: Parking here is a timed session on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

Live Activities

Lock Screen & Dynamic Island

Start and stop Live Activities from Shortcuts or AnyStash. Show a count-up or countdown—with optional location and a reminder when a timed session ends. Built for parking timers, Focus, meetings, and visits.

Event Sessions

Start, end, and history

Record sessions with Start, End, and Checkpoint actions. Browse notes and category filters in the app—a clear run log for shortcuts, errands, and anything you want to review later.

Check In

Location when you need it

One-tap Check In saves where you are (Location When In Use—no continuous background tracking). Browse visit and location history on a map or list.

Data Store

Global variables & files

Keep text, numbers, yes/no, lists, dictionaries, and files in global variables that other shortcuts can Set and Get. Key paths for structured data. On device by default; optional iCloud under your Apple ID.
